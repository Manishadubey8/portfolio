# 🛍️ BuzzBuy – E-Commerce Website

## 📌 Project Overview

BuzzBuy is a modern e-commerce website designed to provide users with a simple and engaging online shopping experience.

The website features a fashion-focused product catalog with different product categories, promotional sections, product pricing, and shopping interactions.

🔗 **Live Website:** [BuzzBuy](https://manishadubey549-code.github.io/buzzbuy/)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🛒 Product browsing
- 🏷️ Discount and sale sections
- 👕 Men's, women's, kids, and accessories categories
- 🛍️ Add to Cart functionality
- 💰 Product pricing and promotional offers
- 📦 Free shipping information
- 💳 Cash on Delivery information
- 📧 Newsletter subscription section
- 📱 Responsive e-commerce layout
- 📰 Latest fashion/trend section

---

## 🛒 Product Categories

The website includes different shopping categories such as:

- Men's Fashion
- Women's Fashion
- Kids
- Accessories

## 🏷️ Products

The website displays a variety of products including:

- Casual Sports Sneakers
- Leather Handbags
- Winter Coats
- Men's Overcoats
- Backpacks
- Umbrellas
- Bracelets
- Jackets
- Boots

Each product includes its name, price, and an **Add to Cart** option.

---

## 🎨 Website Sections

### 🏠 Home

The homepage contains promotional banners, featured collections, product categories, and current offers.

### 🆕 New Products

Displays newly added products with their prices and shopping options.

### 🔥 Best Sale

Highlights products available under promotional offers and discounted pricing.

### 📰 Latest Trends

A dedicated section for fashion trends and latest updates.

### 📧 Newsletter

Users can subscribe to receive updates through the newsletter section.
